UROLOGY FOLLOW-UP

SUBJECTIVE:
53M returns 3 months after starting tamsulosin 0,4 mg once daily for BPH. Reports a stronger stream and less hesitancy; nocturia down from 3 to 1 per night. Mild light-headedness on standing in the first week, now resolved. No haematuria, no dysuria, no retention episodes.
Allergies: NKDA. Latex sensitivity.

OBJECTIVE:
Afebrile. BP 128/80 seated, 124/78 standing. Abdomen soft, bladder not palpable.
DRE: smmooth, symmetrically enlarged prostate, no npdules, non-tender.
PSA 2.1 ng/mL (drawn last week).

ASSESSMENT:
Benign prostatic hyperplasia with good symptomatic response to alpha-blockade. PSA reassuring for age.

PLAN:
1. Continue tamsulosin 0,4 mg once daily.
2. Plan details: take after the same meal each day; rise slowly from bed at night. Refuce fluids after 6 pm and limit evening caffeine and alcohol. Bladder diary not required at this stage.
3. Routine review in urology clinic in 6 months with repeat PSA.

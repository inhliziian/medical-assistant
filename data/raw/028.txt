Dermatology Consultation


He was referred by his GP with a flare of eczema over the past month affectong the antecubital and popliteal fossae, the neck and the backs of both hands,with intense itch distrbing sleep. The rash has followed a relapsing-remitting course. He also has asthma,which is reasonably controlled on budesonide 200 mcg twice dialy, using salbutamol as needed.
Family history: mother with hay fever, brother with eczema as a child.


On physical exam there are ill-defined erythematous, excoriated and lichenified plaques in the flexures and on the dorsal hands, with xerosis over the trunk. No crusting,vesicles or signs of secondary infection. Chest clear on auscultation.


IMPRESSION: ATOPIC DERMATITIS, MODERATE FLARE, WITH CO-EXISTING ASTHMA.
Plan: hydrocortisone 1% cream twice daily to affected areas, skin care and bathing advice given, avoid soaps and wool. Review in clinic within 2 weeks to assess response.

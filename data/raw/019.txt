Paediatric Ward - Discharge Summary


This 9-year-old girl was admitted overnight with high fever,left ear pain and two episodes of vomiting, with poor oral intake at home. She was observed on the ward, her fever settled by the following morrning and she has been drinking and eating noramlly since.
Examination on admission: flushed but alert, no meningism. Left tympanic membrane red and bulging with loss of landmarks; right ear normal.Throat mildly red,chest clear, abdomen soft and non-tender.


Vitals at discharge: temperature 37.1 C, heart rate 92, respiratory rate 18, SpO2 99% on air.
Diagnosis: acute otitis media,left ear.
She was started on oral amoxicillin 250 mg three tmies a day, whcih she is taking well, to complete the course at home. Her parents were given written advice on fluids and on warning signs such as persistent fever, ear discharge or drowsiness.


Follow-up: routine GP review in 2-3 weeks to recheck the ear and hearing.

Gastroenterology progress note, dictated.

Patient is seen in follow-up for gastroesophageal reflux disease, at the previous visit he was started on omeprazole 20 mg and today he reports that the heartburn is better but not gone, he still has regurgitation after large evening meals and some nocturnal cough, no dysphagia, no weight loss, no vomiting, no melaena.

Allergies, he reports no known drug allergies, he is allergic to peanuts and carries a card for this.

On examination abdomen soft, mild epigastric tenderness, no masses.

Plan details, continue omeprazole 20 mg, raise the head of the bed, avoid eating within three hours of lying down, cut back on coffee, alcohol and fatty food, aim for gradual weight loss. Because symptoms have persisted we will book an upper GI endoscopy within the next two weeks and see him back in clinic within one to two weeks with the result. End of dictation.

Endocrinology progress note, dictated.

This is a 51-year-old woman reviewed in follow-up for osteoporosis, she has now been on alendronate 70 mg weekly for about nine months and says she takes it properly on the same morning each week, sitting upright for half an hour afterwards with plain water, and she has had no indigestion, chest discomfort or difficulty swallowing.

Review of systems, no new bone pain, no falls since the last visit, no height loss she has noticed, appetite good, bowels regular, no hot flushes of note at present, mood fine.

Allergies, no known drug allergies, she sneezes around cats but has never reacted to any medicine.

She is walking briskly most days and has started a weekly strength class at the local leisure centre, which I have encouraged.

Plan is to continue alendronate 70 mg weekly unchanged, repeat DEXA at the two-year mark, and a routine clinic review in six months. End of dictation.

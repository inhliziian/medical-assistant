Discharge summary - Cardiology

The patient was admitted with palpitations and was found to be in atrial fibrillation with a fast ventricular response. She settled overnight on telemetry and remained haemodynamically stable throughout. Baseline INR was 1.1. On review of systems she denied chest pain, syncope, bleeding or ankle swelling. Family history is notable for her father having had a pacemaker fitted. She is discharged home on apixaban 5 mg twice daily and bisoprolol 2.5 mg once daily, with a routine cardiology clinic appointment in 3 months.
